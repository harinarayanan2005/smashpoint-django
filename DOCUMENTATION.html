Engineering & Architecture Whitepaper

SMASHPOINT Badminton E-Commerce Platform

Complete Technical Reference, Architecture Breakdown, File Analysis & Flow Diagrams

1. Executive Summary

SMASHPOINT is a full-stack tournament-grade badminton retail platform built on pure Django (Model-View-Template architecture) paired with Three.js (WebGL) hardware-accelerated graphics. It delivers an end-to-end shopping experience featuring 29 authentic products across Yonex, Victor, and Li-Ning, multi-parameter filtering, persistent cart sessions, and an enforced player authentication gate for checkout.

2. System Architecture & High-Level Flow

The platform adheres strictly to Django's Model-View-Template (MVT) design pattern:

3. Database Models (store/models.py)

Data persistence is managed via Django ORM with SQLite backend (db.sqlite3). Below are the four core models and why each is used:

3.1. Category

Defines major store departments (Racquets, Footwear, Shuttlecocks, Bags & Gear). Uses a unique SlugField for SEO-friendly URLs.

3.2. Product

Stores detailed equipment specifications for all 29 products:

  • name (CharField): Full equipment name (e.g. Yonex Astrox 100 ZZ Kurenai).
  • price (DecimalField): Exact financial value in Indian Rupees (₹), preventing floating point rounding errors.
  • image_url (URLField): Stores relative path to image assets (e.g. images/astrox100zz.png).
  • specs (CharField): Weight/Grip class, flex, and tension recommendations (e.g. 4U/G5 • Stiff • 30 LBS).
  • badge (CharField): High-contrast tactical tags (e.g. HEAD-HEAVY, SWORD SPEED, PRO SUPPORT).
  • rating (DecimalField): Real-world customer review scores (e.g. 4.9).

3.3. Order & OrderItem

Handles transactional order storage and Indian postal delivery records:

  • Captures customer details (Full Name, 10-digit Phone, Street Address, City, State, 6-digit PIN code).
  • Records selected payment mode (Instant UPI, Cards / NetBanking, Cash on Delivery).
  • The related OrderItem model stores an immutable snapshot of purchased equipment names, prices, and quantities, preserving financial records regardless of future price changes.

5. Controller Logic & Business Rules (store/views.py)

5.1. home(request, category_slug=None)

The primary view handling catalog browsing, search, and multi-filtering:

  • Category Aliasing: Normalizes incoming category strings (e.g. converting racket to racquets).
  • Multi-Parameter Filtering: Combines Category, Brand (case-insensitive exact match), Budget ranges, and Play Style balance using Django Q objects.
  • Search Algorithm: Queries across 5 fields simultaneously: product name, brand, specs, badge, and description.
  • Sorting: Dynamically sorts products by price ascending, price descending, or customer rating.

5.2. user_register(request) & user_login(request)

Manages player account creation and authentication:

  • Validates duplicate usernames and emails with custom flash messages.
  • Enforces minimum 6-character password rules and confirmation match.
  • Auto-Authentication: Automatically authenticates the player upon registration and immediately forwards them to next_redirect (e.g. straight to /checkout/).

5.3. checkout(request)

Guarded with the @login_required(login_url='/login/?next=/checkout/') decorator:

  • Unauthenticated visitors are escorted to the login/registration gate with a prominent requirement notice.
  • Authenticated visitors receive the shipping address form and payment selector.
  • On form submission, parses shopping bag JSON, creates database Order and OrderItem rows, and displays order_success.html with an itemized invoice.

6. Static Assets & Image Pipeline

How Product Images are Loaded: In models.py, products store relative paths such as images/astrox99pro.png. In templates, the image is loaded via: <img src="/static/{{ product.image_url }}">. Django maps /static/ to store/static/ based on settings.STATICFILES_DIRS.

6.1. The Illuminated Studio Pod Rationale

Badminton racquets are composed of matte black carbon-fiber frames with ultra-fine strings. Placing them on dark backgrounds causes them to blend in and disappear. To solve this, every product sits inside a custom white radial pod (.product-image-box) with subtle ambient drop shadows (filter: drop-shadow(...)), ensuring 100% clarity of frames and string patterns.

7. Real-Time 3D WebGL Wave Simulation (hero_wave.js)

The hero section avoids static pictures in favor of a real-time 3D aerodynamic wave simulation powered by Three.js:

  • Particle Matrix: Generates 5,400 points across a 90x60 grid using THREE.BufferGeometry.
  • Displacement Formula: Multi-frequency trigonometric waves model airflow and high-velocity smash turbulence.
  • Dynamic Vertex Coloring: Wave troughs glow deep electric blue, while wave peaks illuminate in neon cyan and magenta.
  • Mouse Parallax: The 3D perspective tilts smoothly in response to cursor movement using linear easing.
  • Interactive Presets: Users can toggle wave speed and turbulence in real-time between Calm Drift, Tour Flow, and Smash Turbo.
